To mix taurine into a poly(vinyl alcohol) (PVA) and gelatin hydrogel or composite matrix, you must prepare the polymers separately to ensure complete dissolution before blending and adding the taurine. Because taurine introduces highly active amino (-NH₂) and hydroxyl (-OH) groups, adding it changes the viscosity and hydrogen-bonding networks of the solution.
Material Preparation (Step-by-Step)
1. Dissolve the PVA
PVA requires high heat to break its crystalline regions and dissolve fully.
• Dissolve PVA powder in distilled or ultrapure water (typically a 5% to 10% w/v concentration).
• Heat the solution to 90°C–95°C while stirring continuously on a magnetic stirrer.
• Maintain this temperature for 1 to 2 hours until the solution is perfectly clear and homogeneous. Allow it to cool down to roughly 40°C–50°C before mixing.
2. Dissolve the Gelatin
Gelatin is heat-sensitive and will degrade if boiled.
• Bloom the gelatin powder in cold distilled water (typically a 5% to 20% w/v concentration) for 5 to 10 minutes to let it swell.
• Warm the mixture gently to 40°C–50°C while stirring until the gelatin fully liquefies and clears.
3. Dissolve the Taurine
• Taurine is water-soluble. Dissolve your target amount of taurine powder into a small, measured volume of warm distilled water or directly into the gelatin solution at 40°C.
• Note: Keep the taurine concentration optimized (usually below 10–20% of total polymer weight), as excessive taurine can disrupt the final gel network and cause structural beads or defects.

Pass your bulk hydrogel through a fine mesh or use mechanical homogenization to create a microgel paste prior to loading the syringe.
